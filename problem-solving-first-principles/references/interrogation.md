# Genesis 3:9-13: what the text gives

- 3:9 "Where art thou?": location. God does not ask to learn.
- 3:11 holds two questions: "Who told thee?" (source) and "Hast thou eaten?" (act). The skill separates them as method: a Reading.
- 3:13 "What is this that thou hast done?" is put to the woman. The serpent gets no question.
- The text records no answer to "Who told thee?". Silence is a result.
- The answers are data, not findings: hiding and fear (3:10), transfer (3:12-13).
- The verdict comes after the questions. Each answers for their own act (3:14-19).
- Mercy follows the verdict: the promise of the seed (3:15) and the coats of skins (3:21). The aim is restoration (Galatians 6:1), not exposure.
- Not every failure is someone's sin (John 9:2-3).
- The curse lasts "all the days of thy life" (3:17) and ends (Revelation 22:3). Never call friction permanent.
