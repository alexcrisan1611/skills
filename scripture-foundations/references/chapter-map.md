# Foundation chapters

Core: Christ (1 Corinthians 3:11; 15:1-4, "first of all").

A chapter is foundational when it uses foundation language ("beginning", "foundation", "corner stone"), when later Scripture argues from it (Genesis 15:6 in Romans 4:3; Habakkuk 2:4; Psalms 110:1), or when it shows God's own mind (Isaiah 55:8-9; Job 38-41; Romans 11:33-36). The third test is a Reading.

Starter set:

- Who God is: Genesis 1; Exodus 3; 34:5-7; Isaiah 40; 44-46.
- Man, work, fall: Genesis 2-3.
- Covenant and law: Genesis 12; 15; Exodus 20; Deuteronomy 6:4-9; 8.
- Wisdom: Proverbs 1-9; Ecclesiastes 12:13.
- Gospel: Isaiah 53; John 1; Romans 1-8; Ephesians 1-2.
- Practice: Matthew 5-7; 22:34-40; Romans 12:1-2; Philippians 2:1-11; 1 Corinthians 13.

Often misused:

- Hebrews 5:12-6:1, "first principles": the doctrine of Christ, milk to move on from. Not a reasoning method.
- Isaiah 28:16; Ephesians 2:20: the corner stone is Christ, never a value proposition.
- Matthew 25:14-30: faithfulness until the Lord returns.
- Luke 14:28-30: the cost is discipleship (14:33).
- Exodus 18; Acts 6; Nehemiah 1-6: narrative. An organizational use is an analogy.
- 1 Chronicles 12:32: they came "according to the word of the LORD" (12:23).
