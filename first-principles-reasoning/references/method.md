# The climb: finding the missing rung

Symptom, then the missing rung, then the repair:
- One vivid story: no pattern. Count the other cases.
- A pattern with no explanation: no principle. State the rule and a case it predicts.
- "We need more discipline": an axiom with no observation under it. Find the measure and the date.
- Two competent people, opposite conclusions: an axiom they do not share. Name it and test it.
- The same claim, repeated louder: no evidence was added. Repetition is not evidence.

One competitor's launch is one data point about another firm. It is not a pattern, and a launch is not a result.

Forward test: the principle must predict a case that has not happened yet, and say what would falsify it.

Stop points, each recorded with its reason:
1. Cheap to reverse: stop at the principle.
2. Strong pattern, unknown mechanism: stop, and set a review date.
3. A known practice fits: apply it.

An axiom from `business-first-principles` keeps its number and stays a Reading.

A character question ("am I lazy?") goes to the condition of the thinker, with evidence, not to the climb.
