# Limits: working where the method stops

Classify the gap:
- Expensive: buy the information, or price the ignorance.
- Too complex: model the part that matters and name the residual.
- Outside the method: name it, and act on what is revealed (Deuteronomy 29:29).
- Moral: the thinker is darkened (Ephesians 4:18). No method repairs it. Ask God (James 1:5).

Then write the limit into the decision record, with what is known and a review date. If no evidence can reach the question, say so.

Three errors at the boundary: false certainty, total skepticism, activity in place of knowledge.

Where readings go wrong:
- Job 38:2-3 is a rebuke, not an invitation to dialogue. What Job 42:7 commends is Disputed.
- Isaiah 55:8-9 is tied by "For" to the call to repent (55:7). The gap is moral as well as one of knowledge, and it does not cancel God's speaking (55:10-11).
- 1 Corinthians 13:12: partial knowledge is temporary, not worthless. Love acts under it.
- Deuteronomy 29:29 sorts by what God revealed, not by usefulness.
- Romans 11:33-36 is a doxology, not a model.
- Ecclesiastes: settled verdicts, or a search corrected by 12:13? Disputed.
