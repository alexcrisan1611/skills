# Scripture and Golden's model: the limits

- Seeking wealth: "Labour not to be rich" (Proverbs 23:4). "They that will be rich fall into temptation" (1 Timothy 6:9). "He that maketh haste to be rich shall not be innocent" (Proverbs 28:20). Life is not in abundance (Luke 12:15).
- Source: "it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth" (Deuteronomy 8:18), against "My power" (8:17).
- "Imagination" in the KJV is nearly always evil (Genesis 6:5; 8:21; Romans 1:21; 2 Corinthians 10:5). Level 4 stands under Proverbs 16:9 and James 4:13-15.
- Creation order: "God created" (Genesis 1:1) comes before "God said" (1:3). Golden's order is not the text's.
- Proverbs 14:23 sets labour against empty talk. Selling and speaking are labour only when they deliver what they promise.
- Hands-on work is commended: 1 Thessalonians 4:11; Ephesians 4:28; Psalms 128:2. Jesus was "the carpenter" (Mark 6:3); Paul made tents (Acts 18:3).
- Buyer psychology: what men esteem highly may be abomination (Luke 16:15). Do not boast of a false gift (Proverbs 25:14) or "make merchandise" of people (2 Peter 2:3). No stumblingblock before the blind (Leviticus 19:14).
- Conscience: "whatsoever is not of faith is sin" (Romans 14:23).
