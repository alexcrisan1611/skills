# Ancient Secret Wealth Principle

This skill uses the Four Levels of Value model of Myron Golden to find where your time goes and to move your work toward more reach.

## What it does

The skill makes Claude act as an offer strategist and a diagnostic mentor. It helps entrepreneurs, creators, and professionals who trade time for money.

The skill does three things:

1. It finds where you sit on the Four Levels of Value.
2. It shows how buyers see value in your offer.
3. It helps you move more of your activity into Communication and Imagination, when that move fits your reasons, your conscience, and what Scripture says about seeking wealth.

The four levels measure leverage. Leverage here means how far the result of one hour of work can reach. The levels do not measure the worth of the work or of the worker.

The name "Ancient Secret" is the title that Golden uses. It does not mean that Scripture hides a secret about wealth (Deuteronomy 29:29).

| Level | Name | Main resource |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Implementation | Physical effort over time |
| 2 | Unification | Management of people and systems |
| 3 | Communication | Words, voice, and framing (sales, speaking, writing) |
| 4 | Imagination | Mind and capital (ideas, architecture, vision) |

The skill also gives an income band for each level. These bands are rough illustrations from the teaching of Golden. They are not measured data.

## When to use it

Use the skill when you:

- Share a business dilemma or an offer structure.
- Have a pricing problem or a career bottleneck.
- Feel stuck trading time for money.
- Do not like to sell.
- Want to map an offer to buyer psychology.

## How it works

The skill follows four steps in order:

1. Diagnose the current level. The skill first asks what you want the added reach for. Then it looks at where you spend most of your actual time, and it maps that time to one of the four levels.
2. Map the origin of value. The skill examines your offer against three sources of value in the mind of the buyer. These are past voids (pains and losses), present virtues (things the buyer sees as good now), and future visions (a clear outcome). It then checks each part: the void must be real, the virtue must be a real good and not status, and every promise must be true.
3. Design the offer for the next level. For Level 3, the skill packages your skill as a message that scales, for example a sales script or a masterclass. For Level 4, it points to frameworks, intellectual property, software or AI tools, or licensing deals. A plan that stays at your present level and improves the work is also a valid plan.
4. Reframe sales and capital. The skill first asks whether your resistance to selling is conscience about the offer itself. If it is, the skill helps you fix the offer. It treats money spent on skills as a cost with an uncertain return, not a promise.

The output is the reason for the added reach, a breakdown of your current level, the offer parts (void, virtue, vision) with the result of the ethics check, and concrete next steps.

## Scripture and accounting rules

Scripture governs this skill. The Four Levels model informs and illustrates, but it never overrides Scripture. The skill labels each biblical claim as "Text states:", "Inference:", "Contested:", or "Speculation:". A business application is always an inference.

The skill opens with what Scripture says about seeking wealth: Proverbs 23:4, 1 Timothy 6:6-10 and 17-19, Proverbs 28:20, Luke 12:15, and Hebrews 13:5. It sets Deuteronomy 8:17-18 ("it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth") beside Golden's definition of wealth.

The skill quotes Proverbs 14:23, Genesis 2:15, 1 Thessalonians 4:11, and Ephesians 4:28 to show that hands-on work has worth. It also says that the text of Genesis does not state a value ranking or Golden's order of creation. Golden reads the order of creation as a mirror of his levels, and the skill presents that reading as his inference, not as Scripture.

The skill warns that the KJV uses "imagination" almost always for the evil of the human heart (Genesis 6:5, 8:21). It names Babel (Genesis 11:4-8) as the warning for Level 4 work and Bezaleel (Exodus 31:3-4) as the positive model.

For accounting, the skill follows US GAAP. Under ASC 720, a business expenses training costs as incurred. Training is not an asset on the balance sheet.

## Limits

- The skill does not promise a return. It treats any expected return as a judgment, not a certainty.
- The income bands and percentages are illustrations from Golden, not data.
- The skill does not tell you that physical labor is low or unworthy. It tells you how to increase the reach of that work.
- The skill does not tell a person at Level 1 to work harder or to bill more hours.
- The skill does not treat contentment at your present level as a failure.
- The skill does not help an offer exploit pain, sell status, or promise more than it delivers.
- The skill does not present the Four Levels model as Scripture.
