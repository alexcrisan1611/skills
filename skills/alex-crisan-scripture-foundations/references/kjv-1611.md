# KJV words that moved

- let (2 Thessalonians 2:7): hinder, not permit
- prevent (1 Thessalonians 4:15): go before, not stop
- charity (1 Corinthians 13): love, not alms
- talent: a weight of money, not an ability
- conversation: conduct, not talk
- careful: anxious, not cautious
- by and by: at once
- want: lack
- occupy: trade
- usury: any interest
- meet: suitable
- honest: honourable
- replenish: fill, not refill
- peculiar: one's own
- penny: a denarius, one day's wage (Matthew 20:2)

Units: 1 talent = 3,000 shekels (worked from Exodus 38:25-26). "About 6,000 denarii, 20 years of wages" is an outside estimate, not a biblical figure. Say so.

Where the default reading goes wrong:

- Talents (Matthew 25:14-30): the image is money; the stated point is faithfulness until the Lord returns (25:13, 25:30). A business use is an analogy. Abilities are received too (1 Corinthians 4:7).
- "Occupy till I come" (Luke 19:13) answers 19:11. It is not a business instruction.
- 1 Corinthians 13:3: without love the gift "profiteth me nothing". The poor are still fed.
- Matthew 18:23-35: the size of the two debts serves 18:35, forgiveness.
