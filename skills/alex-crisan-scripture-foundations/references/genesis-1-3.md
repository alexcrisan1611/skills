# Genesis 1-3: where readings go wrong

- 1:1: creation from nothing rests on Hebrews 11:3 and John 1:3.
- 1:2 "without form, and void": unfinished, not corrupt. Corruption comes with the fall (Romans 8:21).
- Days 1-3 form, days 4-6 fill. Day 2 has no "good" verdict.
- Length of the days, and the sabbath for Christians: Disputed (Exodus 20:11; Romans 14:5; Colossians 2:16-17).
- 2:15 "dress" and "keep": two duties, given before the fall.
- 2:18-23, roles of man and woman: Disputed (1 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Timothy 2:13).
- 3:6: appetite and beauty are goods God made (2:9). "To make one wise" answers the serpent's offer (3:5; 1 John 2:16). Never a sales method.
- 3:9-13: God asks before he judges, and holds each to account (3:14, 3:17). Adam blames (3:12).
- 3:15: the head wound is final (Romans 16:20).
- 3:17: the ground is cursed, not the man, "all the days of thy life". The curse ends (Revelation 22:3). Never call it permanent.
- 3:21: God makes the covering; the aprons (3:7) were man's. Not a model for self-provision.
- Satan is named by John 8:44 and Revelation 12:9, not by Genesis 3.
