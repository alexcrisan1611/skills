# The organization as a body

Analogy only. 1 Corinthians 12 is about the church (12:27). The head is Christ (Colossians 1:18) and God places the members (1 Corinthians 12:18): a CEO is neither. Scripture outranks the body; say where the analogy breaks. Not medical advice.

B1 one body, many members. B2 none self-sufficient. B3 weaker members necessary. B4 honour the unseen parts. B5 placement by function. B6 suffering shared. B7 growth through joints: inspect handoffs. B8 set points need sensor, threshold, response. B9 pain is signal. B10 emergencies need end dates; the curse ends (Revelation 22:3). B11 never attack self. B12 fear hides information. B13 circulate, don't pool; reserves are no clot. B14 selective barrier. B15 remove waste. B16 input sets output. B17 load builds capacity. B18 load plus recovery. B19 repair at rest. B20 cadence. B21 finite: plan succession. Death is an enemy (1 Corinthians 15:26), not design. B22 redundancy. B23 small leaks kill. B24 breath: never apart from God (Acts 17:25).

Misleads: M1 survival over sacrifice (John 15:13). M2 strength as worth. M3 internal competition. M4 appetite as guide. M5 hiding weakness. M6 pain avoidance. M7 short horizons. M8 adapting to the wrong load.
