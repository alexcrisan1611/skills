# Capital and stewardship: where readings go wrong

Lending law is covenant law for persons. A company is addressed only by inference.

- Talents, pounds (Matthew 25:14-30; Luke 19:11-27): the point is faithfulness until the Lord returns. "Idle capital is sin" is an analogy. "True riches" stand against mammon (Luke 16:11).
- Rich fool (Luke 12:15-21): the sin is covetousness, "not rich toward God". Reserves are not condemned.
- 1 Timothy 6:17-19: the store is built by giving. 6:9 warns those who "will be rich".
- Interest: Disputed. All forbidden (Psalms 15:5; Luke 6:35), only oppressive, or Israel's law. Never from the poor (Exodus 22:25).
- Debt (Proverbs 22:7): a consequence, not a ban. Surety: do not enter it (Proverbs 17:18).
- Release year: pricing it into loans is the sin Deuteronomy 15:9 names.
- Joseph (Genesis 41; 47): God showed the cycle (41:16, 25). The bondage of 47:13-26 is Disputed; do not copy it.
- Concentration: the prophets condemn it (Isaiah 5:8; Micah 2:2).
- Counting the cost (Luke 14:28-33): discipleship. Only "count first" transfers.
- Ecclesiastes 11:1-2: generosity or spread risk, Disputed. No return is certain (11:6).
- Promises to Israel (Deuteronomy 15:6) guarantee no business outcome.
