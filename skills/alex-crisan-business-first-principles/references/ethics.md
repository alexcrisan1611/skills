# Ethics: where readings go wrong

Micah 6:8 test. Answer all three. (1) Do justly: "We owe X to Y because Z." (2) Love mercy: what loyalty gives beyond what is owed. (3) Walk humbly with thy God: what do I claim for myself? Where the answers disagree is the finding. A summary of the law, not a replacement.

Scripture addresses persons. Put every application on a named person.

- Giving: gleaning (Leviticus 19:9-10) and direct gifts (Deuteronomy 15:10; Isaiah 58:7) are both commanded. Never call giving "dependence".
- Wages: due the same day (Deuteronomy 24:14-15). Withholding is robbery (Leviticus 19:13).
- Measures (Deuteronomy 25:13-16): the wrong is two standards. Publish one.
- Gifts blind (Exodus 23:8): draw a bright line.
- Grievance: rebuke in private or forgive (Leviticus 19:17-18; Proverbs 19:11). Never silent resentment.
- Unequal yoke (2 Corinthians 6:14-17): moral, about belief. Business scope: Disputed.
- "Least of these my brethren" (Matthew 25:40): disciples or all in need, Disputed. Galatians 6:10 covers both.
- 1 Thessalonians 4:6: the context is sexual purity. For fraud use Leviticus 19:35-36.
- "We knew it not" (Proverbs 24:11-12): God weighs the heart.
- Promises to Israel (Deuteronomy 25:15) guarantee no profit.
