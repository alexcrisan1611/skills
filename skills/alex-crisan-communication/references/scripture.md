# Scripture behind the rules

No verse here is about copy: each use is a Reading. Say what the verse itself is about.

- Truth, no hype: Eph 4:25; Matt 5:37 "Yea, yea; Nay, nay"; Prov 25:14 "clouds and wind without rain".
- Plain at first sight: 1 Cor 14:9 "words easy to be understood"; Hab 2:2 "make it plain".
- Fitted to hearer: Eph 4:15 "the truth in love"; Col 4:6 "seasoned with salt"; Prov 15:1; Prov 25:11.
- Others first, no flattery: Phil 2:4; Prov 29:5 "spreadeth a net"; 1 Pet 2:17.
- Pleasant yet honest: Prov 16:24; Prov 27:6 "Faithful are the wounds".
- Help, not empty care: Jas 2:16 "be ye warmed and filled".
- Hear first, few words, own fault, keep vows: Jas 1:19; Prov 18:13; Prov 10:19; Prov 28:13; Ps 15:4.

Negative wording (Jesus):

- Existing fear is answered with a reason (Luke 12:32; Matt 14:27 "it is I"); Christ speaks peace by divine authority—copy gives only the fact or deed.
- A "do not" brings its replacement: Matt 6:19-20.
- Invitations are positive: Matt 11:28; Matt 7:7.
- Real trouble is plain: John 16:33; Luke 14:28.
