# Scripture behind legal drafting

No verse here is about Romanian contracts: every use is a **Reading** or analogy. State each passage's own point first, and quote only via `alex-crisan-scripture-foundations/scripts/scripture.ts`.

- **Truth, no fictions, keep vows:** Matt 5:37 (*"Yea, yea; Nay, nay"*); Ps 15:4 (*"sweareth to his own hurt"*); Eccl 5:4-5. Cut fake acknowledgments and unkeepable promises.
- **One just weight (symmetry, prompt pay):** Lev 19:35-36; Deut 25:13-16; Prov 11:1; Prov 20:10; Lev 19:13. Same measure for both parties; no hidden traps or abusive one-sided penalties.
- **Plain terms, boundaries, preserved proof:** Hab 2:2 (*"make it plain"*); Deut 19:15 (two or three witnesses); Gen 23:15-18 (Machpelah purchase: price, borders, witnesses); Jer 32:9-15 (narrative point: God's promise of return; analogy: signed, witnessed, open and sealed copies).
- **Fault vs unavoidable loss:** Exod 22:7-15 (custody, proof of loss, hired risk); Jas 4:13-15 (*"ye know not what shall be on the morrow"*).
- **Lawful authority, peace first, stress-test, wisdom:** Rom 13:1-7; Matt 5:25; Prov 25:8-9; Prov 18:17 (*"his neighbour cometh and searcheth him"*); Jas 1:5.
