# Court-tested Romanian contracts and holdings

Statutory public contracts (fetch via `ro-law.ts lookup`):
- **HG nr. 1/2018 Anexa 1 (`hg1`)**: Clauza 4 (written notices, receipt proof), 53 (BNR + 8 pp), 63.2 (breach notice naming clause + cure period), 64–65 (*reziliere* on breach) vs 66.7 (*denunțare unilaterală* without breach), 66 (*forță majoră*, *cazuri fortuite*, mitigation), 68 (risk allocation, injury indemnity), 70 (amicable step).
- **Ordin ANAP nr. 1.554/2023 Anexa (`anap1554`)**: Clauza 29 (*forță majoră* & *caz fortuit*, CC 1.351), 23.10 (default from due date), 7.2 (5-day amicable step). Ignore its slip mixing *denunțare* with *reziliere*.

Court rulings — won vs lost:
- **Won:** `ÎCCJ II civ. 5141/2018` (automatic *pact comisoriu* needs no extra notice); `ÎCCJ II civ. 2144/2020` (accepted *denunțare unilaterală* operates apart from *rezoluțiune*); `CJUE C-186/16 Andriciuc` (core clause survives L. 193 art. 4(6) when grammatically and economically clear); `CJUE C-621/17 Kiss` (fee clause survives when amount, formula, due date are clear).
- **Lost:** `ÎCCJ II civ. 1461/2021` (delay penalty cannot cover total breach); `ÎCCJ RIL 19/2019` (*clauză penală* void in CIM); `CJUE C-143/13 Matei` (vague unilateral rate change); `CJUE C-191/15` (bare B2C choice of law).
- Never cite `ÎCCJ RIL 10/2020` as merits: rejected as inadmissible.
