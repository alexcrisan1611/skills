# Romanian legal terms of art

- **reziliere** / **rezoluțiune** / **denunțare unilaterală**: *reziliere* ends a successive contract for the future on breach (CC 1.549, 1.554(3)). *rezoluțiune* voids a one-off contract retroactively with restitution (1.554(1)). *denunțare unilaterală* exits without breach, on reasonable notice (1.276). Never call convenience exit *reziliere*.
- **pact comisoriu** (CC 1.553): generic „neîndeplinirea obligațiilor” has no effect. Name exact obligations expressly (1.553(1)). Written default notice (*punere în întârziere*) stating the pact's conditions is required (1.553(3)) unless the clause states termination operates „din simplul fapt al neexecutării” (1.553(2)).
- **clauze neuzuale** (CC 1.203): standard liability caps, unilateral exit/suspension, tacit renewal, governing law, arbitration, or court choice have no effect unless accepted expressly in writing (distinct signature/initials, or un-prechecked step with audit log).
- **forță majoră** vs **caz fortuit** (CC 1.351): default excuses both. Naming only *forță majoră* may exclude *caz fortuit*; state the choice explicitly.
- **limitarea răspunderii** (CC 1.355): never excludes *intenție*, *culpă gravă*, or bodily/health harm.
- **dobândă penalizatoare** / **clauză penală**: OG 13/2011 art. 10 applies CC 1.538–1.543 to penalty interest. Name breach type (CC 1.539). B2B money debt defaults by law (CC 1.523(2)(d)); 60-day cap, never fix invoice date (L. 72/2013 art. 5).
