# Romanian GDPR and L. 190/2018 rules

- Quote only consolidated Romanian GDPR (`02016R0679-20160504`, with 2018 corrigendum on art. 17(3), 21(1)) via `ro-law.ts lookup gdpr:...`. Never quote the uncorrected 2016 OJ text.
- Use statutory term *temei juridic* (art. 13(1)(c)), not *temei legal*. Map one *temei juridic* per processing purpose—never list bases globally.
- When relying on art. 6(1)(f), state the concrete legitimate interest (art. 13(1)(d)).
- Never bundle consent inside T&C acceptance (art. 7(2)); state withdrawal is as simple as giving consent (art. 7(3)).
- Include controller identity and contact, DPO if applicable, recipients, third-country transfer safeguards (art. 13(1)), and retention period with its legal or operational criterion (art. 13(2)(a)).
- List all applicable rights (art. 15-22: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection—clearly separated for direct marketing per art. 21(4)—automated decisions if used, consent withdrawal if applicable) plus the one-month response deadline (art. 12(3)).
- Name the authority in full: *Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal* (L. 190/2018 art. 2(1)(c)); `ANSPDCP` is common shorthand.
- Processing CNP/national ID under legitimate interest requires L. 190/2018 art. 4(2) safeguards (DPO, retention schedule, staff training).
