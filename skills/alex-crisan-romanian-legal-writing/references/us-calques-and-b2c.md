# US calques and mandatory B2C rules

- *„ca atare”, fără garanții explicite sau implicite*: illegal in B2C (OUG 141/2021 art. 6–7, 24; OUG 140/2021). State statutory conformity; in B2B define deliverables and defect remedy.
- *daune incidentale sau consecvente*: no Romanian meaning. Use *prejudiciu direct*, *prejudiciu indirect*, *beneficiu nerealizat* (CC 1.531(2)).
- *prin utilizare confirmați că ați citit și acceptat*: fiction; fails CC 1.203, L. 193/2000 Anexă (1)(b), GDPR art. 7(2). Require express pre-contractual acceptance and *suport durabil* (OUG 34/2014 art. 8(9)).
- *instanța sediului Prestatorului* (B2C): unwritten (*nescrisă*) before dispute (CPC 121, 126(2)); consumer domicile governs.
- Bare *guvernat de legea română* (EU B2C): abusive unless noting consumer keeps mandatory residence-state protections (CJUE C-191/15; Rome I art. 6(2)).

Mandatory online B2C checks:
- Pre-contractual info (OUG 34/2014 art. 6; L. 365/2002 art. 5, 8–9) and pay-button wording (art. 8(2)).
- 14-day withdrawal + button *«retrageți-vă din contract aici»* (OUG 34/2014 art. 9–11^1, in force via OUG 18/2026); digital waiver requires all three art. 16(1)(m) steps.
- Digital changes: valid stated reason, no extra cost, durable-medium notice, 30-day free exit on negative impact (OUG 141/2021 art. 18).
- Ambiguity reads against drafter (CC 1.269(2); L. 193/2000 art. 1(2)).
