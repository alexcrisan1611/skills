# The way

Path: one T3 Code thread, on any device: "capture an idea: …".
1. File it word for word: `.tasks/ideas/<slug>.md` in its project's repository, or the overseeing one for a new product. Commit `[skip ci]`; push.
2. Sharpen: one question at a time until it is plain. Add an agreed Sharpened section; keep their words.
3. Offer the gate: "Screen it now?" Research 3 and 6, draft 2 and 5, ask 1 and 4.
4. A pass becomes a spec, drafted with the owner there. A no moves to `.tasks/trash/` with the date and why (John 6:12).

Idea file. Frontmatter: `name`, `captured` (date), `from`, `for`, `gate: open | refine | owner` (owner: to rule). Body: the owner's words, unpolished; Sharpened; a Gate table (criterion, result, evidence), open questions.

Spec ("the work that they must do").
- Problem: who has it first-hand, and its cost now.
- Core flow: the one path that must work immediately.
- Out of scope.
- Feel: two or three words; the interactions that must feel right.
- Voice: three tone words, three sample lines (button, empty state, error), words to avoid.
- Done: the four layers.

Feel and voice are usually missing.
