# The idea gate

Who checks (Exodus 18:22):
- 3, other products: the agent lists them, with reviews, prices; the owner confirms they fail the people in 1.
- 6, the platform it runs on (system, browser, store): shipped or announced fails; a rival nearby is a watch.
- 2 and 5: the agent drafts, the owner confirms. No stated purpose: draft one as a Guess.
- 1 and 4: the owner rules.
- Two products in one: judge each; a no drops that part; ask for the centre.

Push back:
1. At the first sign of a mismatch (SKILL.md, step 3).
2. Name the criterion and the sign.
3. One question at a time; hear the whole answer (Proverbs 18:13; 20:5). Owner away: list them in the idea file.
4. Let the person find the answer: alignment, not winning.

Agreeing to please is the deceitful kiss (Proverbs 27:6).

Verdict:
- Pass pending owner: agent checks hold; only owner items are open. Only the owner passes.
- Refine: an agent check is open; name what closes it.
- No: one plainly fails; it judges the idea, not the person.

Invite the owner to commit it to God (Proverbs 16:3).
