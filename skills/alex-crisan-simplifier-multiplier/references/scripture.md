# The Scripture behind the pattern: own point first

- Exodus 18:13-26: the bottleneck harms both sides (18:18). Moses stands "to God-ward"; the counsel is conditional (18:23). The stated result is endurance and peace, not growth. An agent does not fear God: 18:21 applies to it by analogy.
- Habakkuk 2:2: the vision is God's, about his judgment and timing. A plain written way is an analogy; it must also be true (2:3).
- Mark 6:39-43; John 6:11-12: the multiplying is Christ's miracle. Only the order transfers: seated in companies, thanks, distribution through the disciples, fragments gathered.
- Luke 14:16-23: a parable of the kingdom. The feast is God's, the invitation is the gospel, the guests are the poor (14:21). Narrowly: the servant could compel because "all things are now ready".
- Luke 14:28-33: the cost of following Jesus (14:33). Only "sit down first" transfers.
- 2 Timothy 2:2: passing on the apostolic teaching, "the same", to "faithful men".
- Acts 6:2-7: the apostles kept prayer and the word. The text does not say delegation caused the increase.
- 1 Corinthians 3:6-7; Psalms 127:1: about ministry. Neither planter nor waterer owns the result.
