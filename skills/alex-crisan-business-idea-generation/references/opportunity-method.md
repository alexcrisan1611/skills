# Opportunity method: thresholds

Sizing. Three layers: total, reachable, paying. Add a floor case. Count; do not quote. No sizing method is accurate: state the error range.

Falsify before you build. Four questions, each with a number:
1. Change: what measured fact shows the constraint moved?
2. Workaround: what do they spend on it today?
3. Channel: what does one customer cost to reach?
4. Ground: why us, and what proves it?

First step. It tests the riskiest assumption, produces a number, has a date, involves a paying customer, can be reversed and creates no continuing obligation.

Stop bands, written first. Example: continue at 30 or more paid jobs, extend once at 12 to 29, stop at 11 or fewer. A band with no "stop" is not a test.

Cycle position:
1. Name the driver.
2. Pick a measurable quantity.
3. Price the action against the cycle for each phase.
4. Set the policy in advance.
5. Name the cost if the cycle does not turn.

Remade test: say the offer in plain words; name the older thing that does the job; say what changed; ask who tried before and why they stopped.
