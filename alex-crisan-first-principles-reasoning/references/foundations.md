# Foundations: where readings go wrong

- The regress of reasons stops at God, who is before all things (Psalms 90:2; Genesis 1:1). Creation has a stated reason: "for thy pleasure" (Revelation 4:11).
- Hebrews 5:12 "first principles": the milk of the doctrine of Christ (6:1-2). The method is an analogy only.
- The Greek word (stoicheion) is also "rudiments of the world" (Colossians 2:8) and "weak and beggarly elements" (Galatians 4:9). The word proves nothing.
- Isaiah 28:16 is about trust in God's stone, set against "we have made lies our refuge" (28:15). It sets no pace for reasoning.
- Luke 14:28-30: the cost of following Jesus (14:27, 33). Planning is an analogy.
- Proverbs 8:22 "possessed": possession or creation, Disputed.
- Method without the fear of the LORD (Proverbs 1:7): knowledge on the wrong floor, or no knowledge? Disputed. Both agree the floor is wrong.
- 1 John 4:1 tests claims of inspiration; a wider use is a Reading.
- The mind is the instrument under repair. The damage is moral (Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 1:21; Ephesians 4:18), the repair is God's (Titus 3:5), and the method cannot audit the heart. Ask (James 1:5).
