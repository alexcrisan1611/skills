# Private legal check of samples S1-S6 (hold until Alex has marked)

Source: research/romanian-legal-writing-sources.md (all fetched 2026-11-14).

- S1.3 "30 de zile de la data emiterii facturii": fine B2B (L. 72/2013 art. 5(1) caps at 60 days). No clause fixes when the invoice is issued, which is good: art. 5(3) voids such a clause.
- S1.4 "penalități de întârziere 0,1% pe zi": ambiguous, clauză penală (CC 1.538) or dobândă penalizatoare (OG 13/2011)? Different regimes (open question 6). The text has no "de drept în întârziere" clause, but B2B money debt is in default by law (CC 1.523(2)(d)). Also no cap, and no "fără a fi necesară punerea în întârziere".
- S2.1 names only forță majoră. Under CC 1.351(1) the default excuses both forță majoră and caz fortuit; by naming only one, the clause may be read as excluding caz fortuit ("părțile nu convin contrariul"). The drafting choice should be explicit.
- S2.2 the definition is quoted correctly (CC 1.351(2)). OK.
- S2.4 "rezilia" fits a services contract with successive performance. If the deliverable is one-off software, the act may be rezoluțiune. Check the object. Also CC 1.557: total and definitive impossibility ends the contract de plin drept anyway.
- S3.1 "reziliat ... cu un preaviz": a convenience exit is a denunțare unilaterală (CC 1.276), not reziliere (CC 1.549, a remedy for breach). Wrong term of art.
- S3.2 pactul comisoriu in general terms ("nu își îndeplinește obligațiile contractuale") has no effect: CC 1.553(1) requires the obligations to be named expressly. The notice must state the conditions (1.553(3)).
- S3.3 matches CC 1.554(3). "Rezilierea" also used for S3.1's exit, which is the term-drift problem: S3.1 and S3.3 use one word for two concepts.
- S4.1-S4.3 in B2C T&C: standard terms limiting liability need express written acceptance (CC 1.203). They cannot cover intent or gross negligence (CC 1.355(1)). They can be abusive under L. 193 Anexă (1)(h). "Pierderea de date" excluded outright is risky. The words "incidentale sau consecvente" are a calque of US "incidental or consequential" with no Romanian legal meaning; RO law has "prejudiciu direct / indirect", "beneficiu nerealizat" (CC 1.531(2) "beneficiul de care acesta este lipsit"). "Ca atare" / "fără garanții ... implicite" is a US disclaimer that conflicts with the consumer's statutory conformity rights (not fetched: check OUG 141/2021 / L. 449/2003 successors).
- S4.4 acceptance by use: fails CC 1.203 "în mod expres, în scris" for neuzuale clauses, and L. 193 Anexă (1)(b). Also "confirmă că a citit, a înțeles" is a fiction, and GDPR art. 7(2) bars bundling consent.
- S5.2 bases listed globally, not mapped per purpose (GDPR art. 13(1)(c)). The legitimate interest isn't named (art. 13(1)(d)). "Temeiul legal" vs the regulation's "temeiul juridic" (art. 13(1)(c)): pick the regulation's term.
- S5.4 a retention of "3 ani" with no criterion; art. 13(2)(a) is met formally, but the reason (limitation period?) is missing. The controller's identity and contact are missing from the S5/S6 excerpt (art. 13(1)(a)); that may be only an excerpt artifact.
- S6.1 missing: the right to withdraw consent (only if a consent basis exists), art. 22 (automated decisions) if relevant, the one-month answer deadline (art. 12(3)).
- S6.3 the authority's full name matches L. 190/2018 art. 2(1)(c). "ANSPDCP" is not in the law; fine as the common acronym, but not a statutory term.
