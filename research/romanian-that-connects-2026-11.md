# Romanian that connects: source research (2026-11)

Reviewed: 2026-11-14 · Review again by: 2027-05-14

Input for the future skill `skills/alex-crisan-romanian-that-connects/`. This file collects sources. It sets no rules.

**Labels used on every item**

- **Sourced**: the cited source says it.
- **Observed**: I saw it in real copy. URL and date checked are given.
- **Inferred**: my own reading. Treat it as a hypothesis for the native reader to confirm or reject.

**How quotes are rendered**

- Quotes I extracted myself from the page or PDF text (DOOM3 study, dexonline, the Microsoft style guide, the România literară archive, the Diacronia PDFs, the landing pages) are verbatim.
- Quotes marked *(via fetch summary)* came through a summarizing fetch tool. The wording is probably right but should be checked against the page before anyone reuses it.
- Several older sources write ş/ţ with a cedilla (U+015F/U+0163). I transcribed those quotes with comma-below ș/ț and flagged them **[cedilla in source]**. Where the cedilla itself is the evidence (section 1.6), I kept it verbatim and flagged it.
- Dilema veche and Rodica Zafiu write with the pre-1993 spelling î-inside-words (*sînt*, *întîmplare*, *rămîne*). I kept that spelling in quotes and flagged it **[î-spelling in source]**. DOOM3 norm: *sunt*, *â* inside words.

All URLs were checked on 2026-11-14 unless stated otherwise.

**Inferred summary of Zafiu's position, for the skill author:** loans bother her less than calqued structures. Corporate language becomes irritating through obligatory clichés, the same mechanism as *limbă de lemn*. She describes rather than bans, and notes that DOOM records forms she still calls barbarisms (*a prioritiza*). That gap matches DOOM3's own "inclusion ≠ recommendation" (1.1.3).

Not fetched: her România literară columns on *provocare* (nr. 27/2003) and *a focusa* (nr. 7/2007), both cited in [S11]; „Topica și topicul" (2003) on *topică* = "topic"; „Datorie suverană" (2012).

**Inferred pattern (for the native reader to test):**

- Every Romanian SMB-facing product I sampled uses *tu* on marketing and help surfaces: invoicing (SmartBill, Oblio, FGO, Facturis), accounting (Keez, SOLO), booking (MERO, Fresha), and marketplace and e-commerce (eMAG, Shopify).
- *dvs.* survives in legal and consent lines, notices, user testimonials and Microsoft-style UI buttons. Several pages mix the two registers.
- Plain-language promises are a recurring selling point: „fără legaleză" (SOLO), „fără limbaj tehnic" (Keez), „fără bătăi de cap" (MERO).
- Where these products name the reader's company, they say *afacerea ta* / *firma ta*, not *business-ul tău* (SmartBill, Oblio, Keez, FGO, Shopify, eMAG Marketplace). Observed.

## Open questions

1. **Register preference is unmeasured.** I found no survey or UX study of Romanian SMB owners on tu vs dumneavoastră. The sampled products all use *tu*, but that shows what vendors do, not what buyers prefer. Worth testing with real clients, by age and sector (salon owner vs accountant vs SRL administrator).
2. **In-app microcopy and transactional emails were not checked** for SmartBill, Oblio, FGO, Keez, MERO or Fresha. That needs accounts. Button register (the plural imperative *Salvați* vs the singular *Salvează*) is exactly where Microsoft and the Romanian SMB tools may diverge.
3. **Not verified:** Revolut RO and Banca Transilvania (HTTP 403), Booksy RO (JS-only page) and Stripe RO (served English). The Microsoft Terminology database was not queried.
4. **Dictionary vs critic conflicts** for the native reader to settle with real samples: *a aplica* (DEX '16 accepts it), *a realiza* = "a-și da seama" (DEX '09 accepts it), *locație* = "loc" (DEX '09 and DOOM3 accept it), *suport* = "sprijin" (DOOM3 *suport²*), *a focusa / a prioritiza / a impacta* (DOOM3 lists them; Zafiu and dexonline reject them).
5. ***la finalul zilei*** has no normative or linguist source calling it a calque. Its listing here is my inference.
6. ***eligibil*** for offers and programmes is missing from the dictionaries checked, yet Google RO uses it. Does it still read as a calque to SMB owners, or as plain administrative Romanian (EU-funds vocabulary)?
7. **No diacritics** on SmartBill and Oblio marketing pages: do SMB owners notice, and is it read as careless or as normal informal style?
8. **AI vs IA**: business media write *AI*; Shopify mixes both; DOOM3 has neither abbreviation. Is there an Academy or Institute recommendation? Not found.
