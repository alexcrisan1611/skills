# Hebrew and Greek: limits

- The `def` line is the lexical claim. The KJV gloss list shows what the translators chose, not what the word means.
- Write "H7287 is glossed X" and "therefore Y" as two sentences. Label the second Reading.

Words usually overread:

- radah H7287, dominion (Genesis 1:26): rule. The root "tread down" does not make it harsh: Leviticus 25:43 must add "with rigour", and Solomon's dominion had peace (1 Kings 4:24). Neither "exploit" nor "gentle tending". Its object is creatures and earth, not people.
- bara H1254: the verb alone does not prove creation from nothing. Hebrews 11:3 and John 1:3 do.
- yom H3117: literal and figurative senses. The lexicon settles neither view of the days.
- ezer H5828, help: also used of God (Psalms 121:2), so no lower worth. Roles: Disputed.
- nephesh H5315: "a breathing creature". Soul apart from body: Disputed (Matthew 10:28; 2 Corinthians 5:8).
- teshuqah H8669 (Genesis 3:16): "a longing". Its direction is Disputed (Genesis 4:7).
- stoicheion G4747, "first principles" (Hebrews 5:12): the doctrine of Christ, called milk. Negative in Colossians 2:8. Not a reasoning method.
- charis G5485: grace creates no debt (Romans 11:6).
