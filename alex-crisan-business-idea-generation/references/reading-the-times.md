# Reading the times: tests and limits

No verse gives a trend method. This is a synthesis with an unknown error rate. Business cycles: Disputed.

Durable change or fashion? Seven checks:
1. Did a constraint change?
2. Who paid?
3. Does it survive a downturn?
4. Do independent sources agree?
5. Did you see it in the field, or read a report?
6. What would reverse it? Write that before you commit.
7. What follows second?

A claim about the future:
1. The forecaster's earlier record: necessary, not sufficient (Deuteronomy 13:1-3).
2. The source: disclosed (a published rule, a signed contract) or deduced?
3. A threshold that would prove it wrong.
4. Does the forecaster lose standing on failure?
Keep a forecast log.

Limits:
- Daniel 2: the stone is God's kingdom (2:44). Only "a position is temporary" transfers.
- Isaiah 41-46: declaring the end is God's mark, not a method.
- Proverbs 27:1 forbids the boast, not the plan.
- Ecclesiastes 9:11: reserves hedge "time and chance", not the trend.
- Prophecy today: Disputed. A business forecast is never a disclosure from God.
