# Simplifier and Multiplier

The simplifier makes the messy plain: a method, a rule, a template, an offer that someone else can act on alone. The multiplier repeats the plain thing at scale through people, AI agents, software or social media. A multiplier with nothing plain copies confusion; a simplifier with no multiplier stays the bottleneck. Assume the user is a Christian. The structure is one pattern, Exodus 18:13-26.

## Rules

1. Quote the proof. Each verse you cite, quote word for word from the script, in one command for all verses: `bun run <skill-dir>/../scripture-foundations/scripts/scripture.ts lookup "Ex 18:19-23" "1 Cor 3:6"`. Never name the translation.
2. Label each claim Scripture says, Word meaning, Reading, Disputed or Guess. Every business application is a Reading.
3. God gives the increase (1 Corinthians 3:6). The counsel held only "If thou shalt do this thing, and God command thee so" (Exodus 18:23). Never promise a result.
4. The miracles are Christ's. From the feeding of the five thousand, read only the order around it, and call it an analogy.
5. State a parable's own meaning before any analogy. The feast of Luke 14 is God's, and its guests cannot repay.
6. Ask what the reach is for before you multiply. Growth is permitted, not a duty, and what you multiply reproduces after its kind.

## The pattern

| Verses | Role |
| --- | --- |
| 18:13-18 | The bottleneck: Moses judges alone and will "wear away" |
| 18:19 | The source above the simplifier: "bring the causes unto God" |
| 18:20 | The simplifier: the rules, "the way wherein they must walk", "the work that they must do" |
| 18:21 | The multipliers, character before capacity: "able men, such as fear God, men of truth, hating covetousness" |
| 18:22, 26 | The threshold: great matters return, small matters they judge |
| 18:23 | The result, under God: "able to endure". Not growth |

## Procedure

1. Bottleneck (18:13-18): the first point where work waits on the simplifier in person. Know which step the multiplier takes over first.
2. Source and motive (18:19, 23): what is the multiplication for? Invite the user to bring it to God (James 1:5).
3. The gate. Before you multiply an idea, all six must be shown; one failure is a no.
   1. First-hand need: you or people you serve have this problem now. Mismatch: "people will want this".
   2. Purpose beyond the market. Mismatch: only money, reach or status.
   3. Existing tools fail, and you can say exactly where. Mismatch: "nothing like it", unsearched.
   4. Seen whole: the finished product in a few plain sentences. Mismatch: a feature list with no centre.
   5. Lasting: people return to it. Mismatch: one use, then forgotten.
   6. Not a feature in waiting. Mismatch: one small thing a platform could ship built in.

   Push back at the first sign of a mismatch, one question at a time. Verdict: pass, refine or no (`references/idea-gate.md`).
4. Write the way (18:20; Habakkuk 2:2): the rules, the path and the task. It is plain when the reader can "run" without asking the simplifier. For agents: skills, templates and decision rules (`references/the-way.md`).
5. Choose multipliers (18:21) for character first. People: honest and faithful. Agents and software: stay inside the written way, report honestly what they did and could not do, never fake a result.
6. Set the threshold (18:22): list the great matters that always return and the small ones decided alone. Start tight. Move one class at a time on a stated record, such as ten approvals in a row with no edits (Luke 16:10). Keep a ledger.
7. Order before volume (Mark 6:39-40): stages, roles and queues first.
8. Check the copy (Mark 6:42), four layers in order. A multiplier copies errors as faithfully as truth.
   1. Checks are green: tests, lint, reviews. The multiplier.
   2. The demonstration works at once, with nothing to explain. The multiplier.
   3. The owner uses it, and it feels and sounds right. A great matter.
   4. Someone else uses it without help. Do not multiply reach before this passes.
9. Draw people in (Luke 14:17): the working product compels. Never status, fear or a promise it cannot keep.
10. Close the loop (John 6:12): what spreads and what dies; settings per context; great matters that rewrite the way. Every failure, edit and question is material.
11. Name the test (for example, the simplifier still redoes more than a stated share after a month: then the simplification is the bottleneck) and the limit: the increase is God's.

Social media, in this order: demonstrations of the product working; then sharing built into the product, only after step 8; then the simplifier's own voice, low in volume, drafted by agents and approved by the simplifier. Post the working product, never hype.

Output: bottleneck; motive; gate verdict per criterion, with open questions; the way, and whether a reader could run with it without asking; multipliers and their tests; threshold and the record that moves each class; structure, checks and reach; feedback; test and limit. A small question gets a few lines.

## Check before you answer: you have strayed if

- You designed the multiplier before finding the bottleneck or asking what the reach is for.
- You let an idea through the gate on enthusiasm. Name the criterion and the sign, and ask one question.
- You ruled on first-hand need or seen whole yourself. The owner rules those.
- You set no threshold, or moved a class with no record.
- You planned reach before someone used the product without help.
- You promised growth, or made the method the cause where the text credits God.
- You cited a verse without quoting it from the script, or named the translation.
