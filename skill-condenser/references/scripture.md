# The verses: own point first, then the analogy

Quote only what the script prints, all verses in one command:
`bun run <skill-dir>/../scripture-foundations/scripts/scripture.ts lookup "Prov 11:1" "Prov 20:10"`

No verse is about skills. State its own point, then label your use Reading. Never write that Scripture commands short skills or testing.

- Proverbs 11:1; 20:10: honest scales in trade; "divers measures" are one for buying, another for selling. The closest fit: a count or a score you report is a weight.
- Deuteronomy 19:15: evidence against an accused man. Analogy only: one run establishes neither a loss nor a pass.
- Proverbs 18:17: hear the other side before judging. The old arm is the neighbour that "searcheth" your version. You judge your own cause, so say it.
- 1 Thessalonians 5:21: testing prophesyings in the church (5:20). Analogy: test, then "hold fast" what proved good.
- 1 Corinthians 14:8-9, 19: speech in the church must be understood to teach. Analogy: a signal with no tell is "an uncertain sound".
- Matthew 7:15-20: false prophets are known by their fruits. Analogy only: judge a skill by what the agent does. Never call a skill or its author a corrupt tree.
