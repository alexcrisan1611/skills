# Business Idea Generation

An opportunity is a change in the world that makes some work newly worth doing. Scripture gives cases, not a method for market research: this procedure is a synthesis, and every business use of a text is a Reading. Say "this suggests".

Not for capital, hiring or org design (`business-first-principles`), and not for choosing tools.

## Procedure

Each step names the evidence, then the failure.

1. The change. A change in cost, time, distance, rule or tooling, with a date. Failure: a mood called a change.
2. Who it affects. A named group you can reach. Failure: "everyone".
3. The workaround. What they do today: the spreadsheet, the group chat, the paid substitute. Failure: assuming the current method is nothing.
4. The unstructured part. The boundary nobody drew and the category nobody named. Draw the boundary, name it, then fill it. Failure: features before the category.
5. The size. A counted number with an error range, in three layers (total, reachable, paying), plus a floor case. The floor decides go; the ceiling plans capacity. Failure: a report's number with no method.
6. The channel. A named channel and its cost per customer. Failure: the channel left to chance.
7. The test. Written before the build: a threshold, a date, a stopping rule. It tests the riskiest assumption with a paying customer and leaves no continuing obligation. Failure: drift.

A reading of the market becomes a decision only with an owner, an action, a date, a named reallocation and a stopping rule.

## Output

1. The change and its date.
2. The group and its workaround.
3. The unstructured part: its boundary and its name.
4. The size, with the evidence and the floor case.
5. The channel and its cost.
6. Ethics check: the offer serves a real need, its beauty is true to the use, and it does not sell status.
7. Where the market cycle stands, and the action that works against it.
8. The test: threshold, date, stopping rule.
9. The smallest first step you can reverse.

Label each claim Scripture says, Word meaning, Reading, Disputed or Guess; `../scripture-foundations/SKILL.md` defines them. The script cannot verify a market fact: every market claim is a Reading, a Guess or an invented illustration, and you say which. Quote only what the script printed, in one command for all verses: `bun run <skill-dir>/../scripture-foundations/scripts/scripture.ts lookup "Gen 41:25" "Eccl 11:6"`. A quick "is this idea any good?" gets a few lines on the weakest steps: open no reference and cite no verse.

## Check before you answer: you have strayed if

- You stated a market number without saying it is a Guess or how it was counted.
- You named a trend, not a dated change in a constraint.
- You gave no stopping rule, or wrote it after the plan.
- You used a text as a market method (the harvest, the sower, Athens, the spies, Joseph). State its own point first, then call your use an analogy.
- You credited skill where the text credits God (Joseph, David, Issachar). Restore the subject.
- Your offer works by selling status. Redesign it.
- You promised an outcome. A test is not a forecast.
