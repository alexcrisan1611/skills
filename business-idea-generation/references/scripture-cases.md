# Scripture cases: their own point first

Every case is an analogy for a market. State the text's point before you use it.

- Issachar (1 Chronicles 12:32): reading plus doing. They came "according to the word of the LORD" (12:23); the gift is God's.
- Luke 12:54-56: the fault is inconsistency, and the time missed is Christ's visitation (19:44).
- John 4:35: the harvest is people.
- Acts 17:16-34: Paul preaches Christ and corrects their worship.
- Genesis 2:18-20: God named the need before Adam's naming showed it.
- Genesis 41: God disclosed the cycle (41:25). Joseph did not detect it.
- Genesis 47:13-26: profit from distress. Disputed; not a model.
- Ecclesiastes 11:1-2: an idiom, not a bet count. Generosity or trade: Disputed.
- Nehemiah 2:11-18: inspect before you announce; the ground is "the hand of my God".
- Numbers 13-14: an evil report; the root is unbelief (14:11).
- 1 Samuel 17:34-47: David argues from the LORD's deliverance, not his record.
- Matthew 13:3-23: the seed is the word, not a segment.
- Genesis 3:6: an ethics check. The third channel is the serpent's offer (3:5).
- All plans: "If the Lord will" (James 4:15).
