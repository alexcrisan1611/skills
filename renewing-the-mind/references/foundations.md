# Renewing: where readings go wrong

- Matthew 12:43-45: the house is empty of an occupant, and the passage is about a generation refusing Christ (12:41). Using it for thought patterns is a Reading.
- Romans 12:2 is passive. Obedience is the person's; the transforming is God's. So are Titus 3:5, 2 Corinthians 4:16, Ephesians 4:23 and Colossians 3:10.
- Philippians 2:12-13 holds both: "work out", and "it is God which worketh in you". Whether God's work comes first or the person cooperates with grace: Disputed.
- 2 Corinthians 10:4-5: the weapons are "mighty through God", not a technique. Paul's subject is opponents at Corinth; applying it to one's own thoughts is a Reading.
- Audience: Romans 12:1 addresses "brethren". "The natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit" (1 Corinthians 2:14). Point an unbeliever to the gospel first.
- Proverbs 1:7 is Scripture. That a given school leaves the fear of the LORD out is a Reading that needs evidence. What schooling produces is Disputed. Keep the three apart. Daniel learned Chaldean learning, and God gave the knowledge (Daniel 1:4, 17).
- Manner: a right point made with contempt bears the fruit the patterns condemn (Galatians 6:1).
