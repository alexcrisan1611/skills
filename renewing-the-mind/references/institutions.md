# School, feed, screen: mechanisms and limits

Separate the mechanism from the verdict. A biblical category applied to a modern institution is a Reading. Effect sizes are Disputed. Scripture pronounces on no institution's standing. No conspiracy: incentives align. The answer is foundation and discipline, not withdrawal (Daniel 1:4, 8).

School: knowledge without the fear of the LORD (Proverbs 1:7) is the strong claim. Compliance, age segregation, the curve, bells and outsourced parenting are features of any large institution.
- Obedience needs no stated reason (Romans 13:5; Colossians 3:20). The error is compliance that never learns its purpose.
- Fixed stops are biblical (Exodus 34:21).
- Teachers outside the home appear in Scripture (Galatians 4:2; Acts 22:3). The parent keeps oversight. Disputed.

Social media: comparison as the business (2 Corinthians 10:12), envy, the unsatisfied eye (Proverbs 27:20), itching ears, virtue performed (Matthew 6:1).

Television and advertising: manufactured desire, passive reception, appetite as selling point, repetition.
- Images are not worse than words: the Word was seen (John 1:14), and Christ is the image of God (Colossians 1:15).
- Want test: note when the want arrived. If during advertising, examine it.
- A fast is a diagnostic, never a rule (Mark 7:9).
