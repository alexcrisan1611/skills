# Business First Principles

A skill that reasons about business decisions from 34 axioms drawn from Genesis 1-3, not from convention or "best practice."

## What it does

Most business advice gives patterns: what worked somewhere else. Patterns fail in a new market, after a constraint changes, or when a business declines for no clear reason. This skill gives the agent the axioms under the patterns, and a procedure to apply them to one decision.

The axioms come in four groups that follow Genesis 1-3:

1. The source and the substrate (Genesis 1:1-2). Example: all value is derived, so ownership is stewardship.
2. The method of making (Genesis 1:3-2:1). Example: formation comes before filling, so build the structure before the contents.
3. The rhythm (Genesis 2). Example: building and keeping are equal, so maintenance needs named owners.
4. The fracture and how to operate in it (Genesis 3). Example: friction is structural, so every plan must price the "thorns."

Each axiom quotes its verse from the KJV and states the business consequence. The skill labels each business consequence as an inference (a reading of the text, not a statement of the text). It does not claim that the Bible commands a business practice.

## When to use it

Use this skill for a foundational business choice, for example:

- What to build, or how to structure a company
- Whether a plan is sound
- How to allocate money, cash reserves, or time
- Org design, hiring, pay, or succession
- Pricing, growth, maintenance, or risk
- Why a business is failing
- An ethics decision under pressure
- Any choice where the only stated reason is "everyone does it this way"

The user does not have to mention Scripture. For a factual or tactical question, the skill tells the agent to answer normally and not to force the structure.

## How it works

1. The agent names the decision and its real deadline.
2. It separates what is given (market, capital, team, constraints) from what is chosen.
3. It finds the one or two axioms that govern the decision. If no axiom applies, the decision is not foundational, and the agent uses an ordinary pattern.
4. It writes out the derivation from the axiom to this situation.
5. It names the specific friction and its cost, the owner of maintenance, the boundary, the definition of "finished," the evaluator, and the review cadence with a stopping rule.

That is the full 10-step procedure. For smaller decisions, the skill gives a short form of seven questions. It also lists 12 anti-patterns, each with the axiom it violates and the cost it causes.

The answer follows a fixed structure: the decision, the substrate, the governing axioms, the conclusion, the friction, the keeper and the boundary, and a confidence note. The confidence note separates what the text states from what the agent infers.

## What is in this folder

- `SKILL.md`: The 34 axioms, the 10-step procedure, the short form, the anti-patterns table, and the output format.
- `references/axioms.md`: The full derivation, verse by verse, for each axiom, with an appendix that logs the verification of each claim.
- `references/canon-survey.md`: A wider pass over Scripture on purpose, integrity, wisdom, leadership, execution, money, resilience, and communication. Read it as analogy. Where it conflicts with the axioms, the axioms win.
- `references/capital-and-stewardship.md`: Money, debt, and reserves. It covers the talents and the minas, counting the cost, the seven-year cycle of Joseph, the jubilee, and stewardship.
- `references/org-and-labor.md`: Structure, delegation, hiring, pay, and succession. It covers Exodus 18, Acts 6, Nehemiah, David and Solomon, Bezaleel, and counsel.
- `references/ethics.md`: The Micah 6:8 test, the commerce rules of Leviticus 19, honest measures, the golden rule, and a method to make an ethics call under pressure.
- `references/trends-and-timing.md`: Reading the times (Issachar), cycles, reconnaissance, testing a forecast, and action under uncertainty.

Each reference file labels each claim as "Text states," "Lexical" (about the Hebrew or Greek word, checked against Strong's), "Inference," or "Speculation." The file `trends-and-timing.md` also uses "Contested."

## Dependencies

This skill depends on the `scripture-foundations` skill in the same repository. `SKILL.md` points to `../scripture-foundations/SKILL.md` for the method of derivation and its guardrails. The reference files cite verses from `scripture.ts` and Strong's definitions from `lexicon.ts`.

## Example prompts

- "Everyone in our industry raises a big seed round before they launch. Must we do the same?"
- "We ship new features every month, but churn keeps going up. What is wrong?"
- "How much cash do we keep in reserve, and what is it for?"
- "I want to promote my best engineer to manage the team. Is that sound?"

## Limits

- The business consequences are inferences by a fallible reader. They are not Scripture, and you can disagree with them.
- The skill does not replace legal, tax, or accounting advice.
- The skill does not give market data, forecasts, or financial models. It gives a method to reason about a decision.
- The canon survey is analogy. It does not have the same weight as the Genesis 1-3 axioms.
- The skill is for foundational decisions. For routine tactical work, it tells the agent to use ordinary methods.
