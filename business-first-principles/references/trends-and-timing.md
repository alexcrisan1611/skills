# Trends and timing: where readings go wrong

No passage here gives a forecasting method. Every business use is analogy.

- Ask "is it good?" first (Ecclesiastes 12:14), then "is it its time?" (3:1-8). A wrong act never becomes right in season.
- Joseph (Genesis 41): God revealed the cycle (41:16, 25, 32). Only "store in plenty against a known risk" transfers. Genesis 8:22 promises seasons, not markets.
- Issachar (1 Chronicles 12:32): they came "according to the word of the LORD" (12:23). Gift or skill: Disputed.
- Sower (Matthew 13:3-23): the seed is the word; the thorns are care and riches (13:22), not competitors.
- Daniel 2: the stone is God's kingdom (2:44), not a disruptor.
- Isaiah 28:16: the stone is Christ (1 Peter 2:6). Not a rule against hurry.
- Spies (Numbers 13-14): the facts were right; the failure was unbelief (14:11).
- David (1 Samuel 17:37, 47): his confidence rested on the LORD, not on a track record.
- Forecasters: a record is necessary, not sufficient (Deuteronomy 13:1-3; 18:22).
- Chance (Ecclesiastes 9:11): the LORD disposes (Proverbs 16:33). Success is not proof of favour.
- James 4:13-16 and Proverbs 27:1 forbid the boast, not the plan.
