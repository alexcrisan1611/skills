# Canon Survey — Principles Distilled Across Scripture

## Provenance and status

This file records a second, wider pass over Scripture, filtered for business use. It came from
a separate working session and is kept here because it covers ground the Genesis 1-3 axioms do
not reach: purpose, communication, resilience, and governance.

**The filter used to build it:**

- **Kept** — direct commands about conduct: honesty, fairness, diligence, rest. These transfer
  as they stand.
- **Kept** — descriptions of God's character and long-range action: patience, long horizons,
  forgiveness. These transfer as strategic posture.
- **Dropped** — redemptive events and doctrine with no business analogue: atonement mechanics,
  the resurrection as doctrine, the new birth, covenant specifics, Israel's civil laws,
  apocalyptic timelines.
- **Flagged** — everything here is **by analogy**. No chapter below is a management manual.
  The value is the first principle, never a proof-texted tactic.

Read this alongside `axioms.md` (A1-A34), which carries the primary derivation. Where the two
touch the same subject, the axiom numbering is authoritative.

**Dropped, and why.** Atonement and sacrificial law (Leviticus 16; Exodus 12) are theological,
not managerial. The resurrection as doctrine (1 Corinthians 15) is the same — though 15:58
transfers, and does so strongly. So do the new birth (John 3) and covenant mechanics
(Genesis 15; Jeremiah 31). These shape _why_ a person works with integrity. They do not
generate business method.

## 1. Foundation and first principles

- **Master fundamentals before scaling.** Hebrews 5-6 names "first principles" and then says
  _go on to perfection_. Get unit economics, product truth, and cash discipline right before
  growth — then deepen.
- **The foundation determines what survives the storm.** Matthew 7:24-27. Stress-test the model.
  What is built on real value survives downturns. What is built on hype does not.
- **One non-negotiable core.** 1 Corinthians 3:10-15; Isaiah 28:16; Ephesians 2:20. Every
  business needs one defining value proposition — a cornerstone — that everything aligns to.
- **Your outputs will be audited.** 1 Corinthians 3:13-15. Work is tested, and what does not
  survive is burned. Assume results will be examined. Build materials that pass the fire.
- **Make the foundation reproducible.** 2 Timothy 2:19; 3:14-17. "The foundation of God standeth
  sure" because it is _written_. Scripture is "profitable for doctrine, reproof, correction,
  instruction" — a four-function description of a good SOP and training manual. If it lives
  only in someone's head, it is not a foundation.
- **Build from a blueprint.** Hebrews 11:10 — a city "which hath foundations," whose builder had
  a design. Clear end-state picture before construction.

## 2. Purpose and the long game

- **Vision precedes building.** Genesis 1 is methodical: separate, name, organize, then fill.
  John 1 says the Word was first. Clarity of idea before execution.
- **Work as calling, not just profit.** Ecclesiastes 12:13; 1 Corinthians 10:31;
  Colossians 3:23. "Whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God" — excellence as motive, not
  only margin.
- **Think in generations, not quarters.** Deuteronomy 6:4-9 (teach your children);
  Jeremiah 31:33 (write it on hearts). Succession and culture transmission are commanded
  patterns, not afterthoughts.
- **Long-horizon patience.** Habakkuk 2:3 ("though it tarry, wait for it"); Galatians 6:9
  (reap in due season, if you faint not).
- **Labour is not in vain.** 1 Corinthians 15:58 — a direct antidote to short-termism. The
  compounding payoff arrives late.
- **Do not be conformed to the market's defaults.** Romans 12:1-2. First-principles thinking
  over industry mimicry. Renew the mind rather than copy the competition.
- **Hold plans humbly.** Isaiah 55:8-9; James 4:13-15 ("if the Lord will"). Your five-year plan
  is a hypothesis, not a prophecy.

_See also A30 (mortality makes time the binding constraint)._

## 3. Integrity and trust

- **Behave the same when no one is watching.** Proverbs 1:7; Job 31. "The fear of the LORD is
  the beginning of knowledge." Accountability to a higher authority is the ultimate compliance
  program.
- **Truthful representation.** Exodus 20:16; Proverbs 11:1; 16:11 (honest scales). No deceptive
  marketing, no massaged financials, no misleading metrics.
- **Pay what you owe, when you owe it.** Exodus 20:15; James 5:4. The cry of withheld wages
  "is entered into the ears of the Lord." Payables, payroll, and contract terms are ethics,
  not admin.
- **Keep your word, even at cost.** Matthew 5:33-37. Let your yes be yes. Reputation compounds
  on kept promises.
- **Justice, mercy, humility.** Micah 6:8 — a three-part test for any hard decision. Is it
  fair? Is it compassionate? Am I being honest about my motives?
- **Consistency under pressure.** Habakkuk 2:4 — "the just shall live by his faith." Principles
  that change with market conditions are not principles.
- **Transparency and early confession.** 1 John 1:5-9 — "walk in the light." Admit error early.
  Corrected mistakes are cheaper than concealed ones.
- **Mercy over ritual.** Hosea 6:6; Exodus 34:6-7. Do not sacrifice people to process.
  Forgiveness should be an operating norm, not an exception.

_See also A22-A26 (trust, deception, hidden failure, diagnosis before judgment)._

## 4. Wisdom and decision-making

- **Wisdom is the highest-ROI asset.** Proverbs 1-9; 3:13-16 — length of days, riches, and
  honour are in wisdom's hand. Fund learning accordingly.
- **Seek counsel of many.** Proverbs 11:14; 15:22. Big decisions get multiple independent
  inputs.
- **Quick to hear, slow to speak.** James 1:19. Listening is a business skill. Customer
  discovery before pitching.
- **Question the too-good offer.** Genesis 3. The first bad advice was a half-truth. Verify
  inputs. Run due diligence against incentives.
- **Stay humble about what you know.** Job 38-41 ("Where wast thou when I laid the
  foundations?"); Romans 11:33. Models fail. Keep margin for the unknown.
- **Write the vision plainly.** Habakkuk 2:2-3. Documented, readable, executable, _waitable_.
  Strategy that is not written is not transferable.
- **Curate the information diet.** Philippians 4:8; Colossians 3:1-2. What leadership consumes
  shapes what leadership decides.
- **Market intelligence and contextualization.** Acts 17:22-31. Paul studied Athens, quoted
  their poets, and spoke their language. Know the customer's worldview before the pitch.
- **Hire for character over credentials.** 1 Samuel 16:7 — "man looketh on the outward
  appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart."
- **Use process for disputes.** Acts 15. Hear testimony, debate, decide on principle,
  communicate the ruling. That is a governance model.

_See also A18 (aloneness is not good), A26 (diagnose before you judge)._

## 5. Leadership and people

- **Servant leadership.** Mark 10:43-45; Philippians 2:1-11. Greatness through service. Leaders
  take the low seat and bear the cost.
- **Love as the operating system.** 1 Corinthians 13. Patience with people. No envy (competitor
  obsession). No boasting (founder ego). No record of wrongs (forgiveness culture). Endurance.
  Love outlasts every process.
- **People over process.** Matthew 22:34-40. The second command is "like unto" the first.
  Treat people as ends, not resources.
- **Unity, no silos.** John 17:21; Ephesians 2:19-22 — "fitly framed together." One mission,
  aligned teams.
- **Self-examination before criticism.** Matthew 7:1-5. Remove your own beam first. That is the
  foundation of a real feedback culture.
- **Dignity for every worker.** Job 31:13-15. The servant and the master share a Maker. Equal
  standards, top to bottom.
- **Grace and second chances.** Luke 15; 2 Corinthians 5:17. Recover the lost customer and the
  failed employee where integrity allows. Restoration beats permanent exile.

_See also A18, A21, A29. Full treatment in `org-and-labour.md`._

## 6. Execution and diligence

- **Faith without works is dead.** James 1:22-25; 2:14-17. Execution over intention. Results
  are the evidence.
- **Diligence compounds; get-rich-quick destroys.** Proverbs 6:6-11; 21:5 ("the thoughts of the
  diligent tend only to plenteousness"); 28:20 ("he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be
  innocent").
- **Do it with your might.** Ecclesiastes 9:10. Full effort in the task at hand.
- **Sowing and reaping is a law.** Galatians 6:7-9. Inputs determine outputs. You harvest later,
  and more than you sow. Applies to capital allocation and culture alike.
- **Persistence and the golden rule.** Matthew 7:7-12. Ask, seek, knock. And treat others as you
  want to be treated. That verse is the customer-relationship baseline.
- **Convert opposition.** Genesis 50:20 — "ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto
  good." Post-mortems over blame.
- **Accountability without blame-shifting.** Genesis 3. The first management failure was passing
  the buck. Own the miss.

_See also A4, A11, A25._

## 7. Money and stewardship

- **Money is a tool, not a master.** Matthew 6:24 — "ye cannot serve God and mammon." Profit is
  fuel, not mission.
- **Budget reveals belief.** Matthew 6:19-21 — where your treasure is, your heart follows.
  Follow the money to see actual priorities.
- **Give from the firstfruits, not the leftovers.** Proverbs 3:9-10. Margin, generosity, and
  investment off the top.
- **Give without PR.** Matthew 6:1-4. Resist performative charity.
- **Diversify and keep experimenting.** Ecclesiastes 11:1-6 — "cast thy bread upon the waters";
  sow morning and evening; you do not know which will prosper. Parallel bets, not single-point
  failure.
- **Be cautious with guarantees and debt.** Proverbs 6:1-5; 11:15; 17:18. The surety warning:
  do not underwrite risk you cannot carry.
- **Pay fair wages.** James 5:4. Withheld pay is not only a labour issue.
- **Contentment is risk management.** 1 John 2:15-17. Covetousness drives bad decisions.

_See also A13, A31. Full treatment in `capital-and-stewardship.md`._

## 8. Resilience, rest, and renewal

- **Trials are training.** James 1:2-4; Romans 5:3-5. Pressure produces endurance. Do not waste
  the downturn.
- **Rest is commanded, not optional.** Exodus 20:8-11; Isaiah 40:31. Renewal for those who wait.
  The team that never rests breaks.
- **Hope as strategy.** 1 Peter 1:3, 13 — "gird up the loins of your mind." Mental preparedness
  under uncertainty.
- **Turnaround starts inside.** Ezekiel 36:26-27; Romans 12:2. New heart, then new behaviour.
  Culture change before process change.
- **Reinstitution is possible.** 2 Corinthians 5:17. Identity is not fixed by past failure.

_See also A15 (rest is structural), A28 (fruitfulness continues at a cost)._

## 9. Communication and reputation

- **Salt and light.** Matthew 5:13-16. Visible good work is the strongest marketing.
- **Simple honesty.** Matthew 5:37. Yes means yes. No embellishment.
- **Words build or destroy.** Proverbs; James 1:19. Slow to speak, especially as a leader.
- **Repetition is internal communication.** Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Peter 1:12-15. Train, retrain,
  remind. Once is not enough.
- **Context before content.** Acts 17. Adapt delivery, never substance.

_See also A4 (making is by word), A7 (naming is an act of authority)._

## How to read this file without overclaiming

1. **Analogy, not command.** Isaiah 28:16 is about a foundation stone in Zion. That it teaches
   something about business foundations is an inference, and a modest one.
2. **The filter is a judgement call, and it is fallible.** Whoever drew the line between
   "transfers" and "does not transfer" may have drawn it wrongly in places. The line is stated
   above so it can be challenged.
3. **A verse supports one principle, not every principle you can attach to it.** Do not chain
   unrelated claims onto the same text.
4. **Where this file and the axioms overlap, the axioms win** — they were derived with more
   care and carry the fuller grounding.
5. **Check the word before you lean on it.** The KJV is 1611 English. See
   `skills/scripture-foundations/references/kjv-1611.md` and `original-language.md`.
