# Beyond Genesis 1-3: keep the subject

Everything here is analogy. Where a text and an axiom overlap, the axiom wins. Where the text says God acts, do not make the leader the actor. Where it names Christ, do not put a business there.

- Foundation, corner stone (1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16): Christ. A value proposition is not a cornerstone.
- Hebrews 6:1: leave the first principles and go on.
- Hebrews 11:8-10: Abraham had no blueprint; the builder is God.
- 1 Corinthians 15:58 rests on the resurrection. No promise of financial return.
- Firstfruits (Proverbs 3:9) go to the LORD, not to margin. Verse 10 is no guarantee.
- Matthew 5:16: the aim is the Father's glory, not marketing (6:1).
- Colossians 3:23: the motive is the Lord. Margin follows; it does not replace it.
- Ezekiel 36:26; 2 Corinthians 5:17: God gives the new heart. "Change the culture" is analogy.
- Genesis 50:20: God turns the evil to good, not the leader.
- Isaiah 40:31: strength comes from waiting "upon the LORD", not from rest alone.
- Micah 6:8 ends "with thy God".
- Galatians 6:7-9: you reap what you sow, not more.
