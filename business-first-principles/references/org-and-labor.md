# Organization and labour: where readings go wrong

Acts 6, 1 Corinthians 12, Ephesians 6 and 1 Peter 5 address the church or households. Business use is analogy.

- Delegation (Exodus 18:13-26): small matters truly transfer; Moses keeps the "hard causes". The text does not define "great": name your threshold.
- Authority (Mark 10:42-45): domination is forbidden, not authority (Hebrews 13:17). Jesus stays "Master and Lord" while washing feet (John 13:13).
- 1 Peter 5:2 excludes greed, not pay (1 Timothy 5:17-18).
- Wages are due at day's end (Deuteronomy 24:14-15). Deferral the employer imposes is the wrong.
- Matthew 20:1-16: the householder stands for God. An owner is a steward (1 Chronicles 29:16).
- Eyeservice (Ephesians 6:5-9) and the sluggard (Proverbs 6:6-11): heart and laziness, not only systems.
- Succession (1 Chronicles 28): David received the pattern from God (28:19). A founder must write and test a spec. His disqualification (22:8): Disputed.
- Nehemiah 5: restore the gain at once. The double duty of Nehemiah 4 is for an emergency.
- 2 Thessalonians 3:10: "would not", not could not.
- 1 Corinthians 12:18: God places the members; a manager's placement can be wrong. More honour to the weaker (12:22-25).
